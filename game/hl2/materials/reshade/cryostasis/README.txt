HL2 Cryostasis ReShade dirt textures
====================================

Dirt.vtf, DirtOVR.vtf, and DirtOVB.vtf are lossless RGBA8888 VTF conversions of
the legacy ReShade shader texture assets:

- https://github.com/crosire/reshade-shaders/blob/legacy/Textures/Dirt.png
- https://github.com/crosire/reshade-shaders/blob/legacy/Textures/DirtOVR.png
- https://github.com/crosire/reshade-shaders/blob/legacy/Textures/DirtOVB.png

They are used only by the HL2 Cryostasis postprocess path to match the active
AmbientLight.fx dirt settings from Cryostasis.ini.
